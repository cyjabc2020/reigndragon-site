# Homepage & non-findings prompts

The 8 non-findings illustrations across the site. Replacing the original
amber-abstract images with the unified watercolor dragon series.

Always prepend [`style-guide.md`](./style-guide.md) (Style guardrails +
Dragon character lock) to each prompt.

---

## 1. Homepage hero — `hero.png`

**File:** `public/images/hero.png`
**Aspect:** very wide (21:9 or wider — full-bleed background)
**Section:** Homepage, full-bleed background behind hero copy
**Important:** copy sits over the **left two-thirds** of the
composition — visual interest must be on the **right**.

> A wide cinematic watercolor scene on warm cream paper. On the right
> side of the composition, the teal-blue dragon is curled around a
> luminous yellow-gold orb roughly the size of its body, painted as
> wet-on-wet glowing pigment with soft halation. The dragon is in a
> thoughtful, watchful pose — one claw resting on the orb, head tilted
> as if studying it, eyes alert. From the orb, faint amber threads
> radiate outward into the cream paper, fading into negative space. The
> left two-thirds of the composition is generous quiet negative space —
> soft cream paper with only the faintest blush of warm color, leaving
> room for text overlay. Deep watercolor pigment at the dragon's
> silhouette, paper grain visible throughout. No text, no labels.

**File:** `public/images/behavioral-experiments.png`
**Aspect:** 4:3
**Section:** Homepage Method grid, card 01

> dragons (the same character) gathered in a small circle around a low

> text, no labels.

## 7. Our Story — `our-story.png`

**File:** `public/images/our-story.png`
**Aspect:** 16:9
**Section:** Homepage Our Story
**Caption:** *Governance is not a manifesto. It is an operating system.*
**Visual idea:** a dragon laying down working infrastructure rather than
proclaiming.

> Watercolor illustration on warm cream paper, wide composition. The
> teal-blue dragon kneels on the ground in profile, carefully laying
> down small modular building-block shapes in a long horizontal row
> across the composition. Each block is painted as a soft cream-and-
> warm-gold rectangle, slightly different from the next — they fit
> together like a foundation being built. Some blocks are already
> placed and stable; the dragon holds one new block in its claws, about
> to set it in place. Above the row of blocks, the cream paper extends
> generously into negative space — calm sky, no clutter. The dragon's
> expression is patient and absorbed. The mood is *building, not
> declaring*. Soft watercolor washes, paper grain, no text, no labels.

## 8. Research hero — `research-hero.png`

**File:** `public/images/research-hero.png`
**Aspect:** 1:1 (square)
**Section:** `/research` page hero, alongside the title

> Watercolor illustration on warm cream paper, square composition. The
> teal-blue dragon sits in a thoughtful, library-like pose — perched on
> a low stack of warm-cream books or scrolls. Its tail curls around the
> base of the stack. Around the dragon, in soft negative space, float
> four small floating watercolor objects: a tiny brass scale (welfare),
> a tiny scroll with faint gold marks (formal theory), a small ring of
> dragon-figure silhouettes (behavioral experiments), and a tiny dial
> (policy-as-product) — each is just a soft pigment suggestion, not
> detailed. They orbit at a respectful distance like attendant ideas.
> The dragon looks slightly upward as if mid-thought. Square format,
> balanced composition, no text, no labels.
